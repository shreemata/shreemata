💡 Accounting & Invariance Principles:

  • Virtual Referral "Take Money": When an owner transfers funds from a Virtual Referral node into their VIP Master Card, funds move between internal categories. The total Need to Pay does NOT change, and Paid Amount does NOT increase.
  • Approved Withdrawals without Bank Settlement: If an admin approves a withdrawal manually without external bank/gateway UTR, the funds remain in Total Need to Pay as Unresolved Settlement Exposure to prevent understating liabilities.
  • External Settlements: An obligation is considered settled ONLY upon verified external payout transfer (e.g. Razorpay payout with transfer ID or bank UTR).
  • Refunds / Reversals: If an admin rejects or refunds a withdrawal, funds return to the customer's normal wallet, preserving exact balance reconciliation without double-counting.
